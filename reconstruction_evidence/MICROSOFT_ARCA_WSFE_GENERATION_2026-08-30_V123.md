## Production boundary

This is a reproducible transport client only. Production still requires a modern WSAA credential service, hardware/managed key custody, ticket refresh fencing, ARCA authorizations and homologation, approved tax/document rules, durable invoice state, idempotency/CAE reconciliation, privacy/audit controls and project evidence. A project is not production-ready until CDN/WAF, IdP, providers, PostgreSQL/recovery, load, offensive security, deployment/rollback and business acceptance are demonstrated.
